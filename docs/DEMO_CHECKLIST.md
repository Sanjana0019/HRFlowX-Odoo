# HRFlowX — Live Presentation & Demo Checklist

This document provides a step-by-step presentation script for showcasing HRFlowX to evaluators and stakeholders.

---

## 🎬 Part 1: Admin & HR Leadership Flow

1. **Admin Login**:
   - Sign in as `admin@hrflowx-demo.com`.
   - Verify landing on the **Admin Executive Dashboard**.
2. **Dashboard Review**:
   - Highlight active employee headcount, daily present count, pending leave approvals, and total monthly payroll.
3. **Employee Directory**:
   - Navigate to **Employees**. Search for an employee (e.g. "Alex").
   - Click to open the detailed profile showing tabs: *Profile*, *Resume*, *Private Info*, *Salary Structure*, *Leave Balances*, *Documents*.
4. **Attendance Management**:
   - Navigate to **Attendance**. View company-wide punch logs and approve/reject pending attendance correction requests.
5. **Leave Approval**:
   - Navigate to **Leave Management**.
   - Review pending leave applications and click **Approve**.
6. **Payroll Processing**:
   - Navigate to **Payroll**.
   - Run batch payroll or view calculated salary slips for the active month.
7. **Document Vault**:
   - Navigate to **Documents Vault**.
   - View secure employee documents and verify signed URL generation.
8. **Analytics & Audit Logs**:
   - Open **Analytics** to show live department charts and attendance breakdown.
   - Open **Audit Logs** to view timestamped administrative actions.
9. **Admin Logout**:
   - Click profile dropdown and select **Sign Out**.

---

## 🎬 Part 2: Employee Portal Flow

1. **Employee Login**:
   - Sign in as `employee@hrflowx-demo.com`.
   - Verify landing on the **Employee Self-Service Dashboard**.
2. **Biometric Punch In**:
   - Click **Punch In** button with geolocation verification. Observe active working timer start.
3. **Leave Request Submission**:
   - Navigate to **Leave / Time Off**. View current balances (Paid, Sick, Unpaid).
   - Click **Apply Leave**, select dates, enter reason, and submit.
4. **View Payslip**:
   - Navigate to **Payroll / Salary**. View monthly earnings, deductions, and download/print payslip.
5. **Notifications**:
   - Click the Notification Bell icon. Review real-time push alert for leave approval or system announcement.
6. **Employee Logout**:
   - Click **Sign Out**.
